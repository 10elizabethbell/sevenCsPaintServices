# Product: Seven C's Paint Services LLC website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages. Only external resource: Cal Sans from Google Fonts.
- **Source:** Muse brief, 2026-10-20 (Facebook group post in "Ocean county small businesses and services.", and the business's Facebook page).

## Users
- Homeowners in and around Toms River, NJ who need interior or exterior painting. They mostly arrive on a phone, from a Facebook link, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner (lead painter, name unknown), who will receive the site for free and judge it in one phone screen.

## Purpose
Turn a visitor into a free-estimate request: a pre-written text message to 732-305-5003, or a call.

## Positioning
"Small family owned and operated painting company out of Toms River. Lead painter brings 29 years of experience to every job. Recent local referrals upon request. Interior and Exterior." (their words, Facebook, 2026-10-20)

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 732-305-5003 (accepts texts: **unknown**), email sevencspaint@gmail.com (footer only), Facebook page.
- No street address published: show "Toms River, NJ" only.
- No reviews, prices, hours, owner name, insurance or licence info on record; the site must say nothing about them.
- Phone-first; no sticky bottom bar; phone number only top-right on the first screen.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: navy (#06204e) script "Seven C's" with gulls, a brush and waves, "PAINT SERVICES" (from the Facebook profile image). Nautical: Seven C's = the seven seas.
- Accent brass (#e8b04a) is **inferred**; the logo has no warm colour.
- Voice: plain, family business, experience-led; "we / our".

## Evidence on hand
- Two before/after pairs from the group post (a hallway with wainscoting, a den with a panelled wall), each about 356px wide.
- 29 years of the lead painter's experience (their claim); "recent local referrals upon request" (their claim).

## Principles
1. The owner sees their own business, done better than they imagined, in one phone screen.
2. Only facts from the brief; inferences are marked in HANDOFF.md.
3. The free estimate is the one action; everything else supports it.
